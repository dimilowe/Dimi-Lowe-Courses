Step 2: Add these blocks (example structure)
You don’t need perfect copy yet — just real, human explanations.
deepContent
This is SEO + narrative explanation.
Example:
The Universal Shopping Cart lets you save products from any website in one place. Instead of bookmarking items across dozens of stores, you can paste a product link and instantly capture its name, image, description, and price. This makes it easier to compare options, track ideas, and plan purchases without losing links.
Explain:
what problem it solves
who it’s for
why it’s better than bookmarks
contentGuide
This is how the tool works, step by step.
Example bullets:
Copy a product link from any website
Click “Add Product” and paste the URL
Review the fetched details and add a price, notes, or tags
Save the item to your Universal Shopping Cart
Open the original store anytime to complete your purchase
This section dramatically improves trust + clarity.
proTips
This is power-user value.
Examples:
Use tags like “Black Friday” or “Compare” to group items
Save price-at-time so you can spot deals later
Share your cart with friends to get opinions before buying
Keep one cart per goal (outfits, home, gifts)
faq
Answer obvious objections:
“Can I buy directly from this cart?” → No, it links you to the original store
“Does it work on any website?” → Yes, any public product page
“Is my cart private?” → Yes, unless you share it
“What happens when I hit the free limit?” → Upgrade to Shopping Cloud Starter for unlimited saves
