Is “free up to 10 items” the right gate?
Yes.
It’s simple, intuitive, and value-aligned.
Users instantly understand:
“This is my universal cart — I can try it for free, but if I actually use it seriously, I pay.”
No dark patterns. No confusion.
The correct gating model (don’t overcomplicate)
Free (no cloud subscription)
Save up to 10 items total
Edit notes + tags
Open merchant links
Delete items freely
Public sharing off (optional — see below)
Shopping Cloud – Starter Tier
Unlimited saved items
Unlimited carts/lists
Shareable public carts
Priority metadata fetch (future)
Price alerts (future)
Early access to extensions (future)
This cleanly maps value → upgrade.
The most important architectural rule
Gating must be enforced at write-time, not read-time.
What that means:
Users can always view their saved items
Users can open links and delete items
Only creating a new item is blocked once they hit the limit
Blocking reads feels hostile.
Blocking writes feels fair.
How to implement this without breaking your system
1. Do NOT hardcode “10” anywhere in the engine
The limit must come from:
Tool metadata
Or cloud entitlement config
Or a centralized usage-limit helper (preferred)
Example conceptually:
maxSavedItems = userHasShoppingCloud ? Infinity : 10
But implemented via your existing entitlement system, not inline logic.
2. The engine should expose usage metrics, not policy
The engine’s job:
Count how many saved items exist for the current user/cart
Expose that count to the UI and API
The policy decision (“allow or block save”) should flow from:
getToolLockState() or
your existing usage/limit helpers
This prevents future drift when tiers change.
UX: how the upgrade should feel (important)
At 7–9 items
Soft warning:
“You’re almost at your free limit (7/10). Upgrade to keep saving.”
At 10 items
Block Add Item
Show upgrade modal immediately
Explain benefit in one sentence:
“Starter unlocks unlimited saved items and shareable carts.”
No long pricing tables. No guilt.
Should public sharing be gated?
Yes — but softly.
Reason:
Sharing has viral upside
It’s also a “power user” behavior
So:
Free: private carts only
Starter: public/shareable carts
This gives you a second upgrade trigger that isn’t just “more slots.”
