One more blunt point (so you don’t get stuck)
On Autoscale, /run must be short. If a video has 300 shots, extracting all frames in one request might timeout.
So in frames stage, make it resumable:
store meta_json.frames_done_count
each /run processes the next N frames (like 25)
when done, advance stage
