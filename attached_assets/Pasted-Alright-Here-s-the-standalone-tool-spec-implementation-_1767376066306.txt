5) Core Processing (FFmpeg filter)
This is the exact “CapCut vertical blur fill” effect (no mirror).
Default config
target_w=720, target_h=1280
fps=60
blur_sigma=35
bg_scale=1.5
bg_dim=0.08
bg_saturation=0.90
Filter (no mirror, blurred zoom background)
ffmpeg -y -i "input.mp4" -filter_complex "
[0:v]split=2[main][bg];

[bg]
scale=${target_w}:${target_h}:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,
crop=${target_w}:${target_h},
gblur=sigma=${blur_sigma},
eq=brightness=-${bg_dim}:saturation=${bg_saturation}
[bgout];

[main]
scale=${target_w}:${target_h}:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease
[mainout];

[bgout][mainout]
overlay=(W-w)/2:(H-h)/2:format=auto
" -r ${fps} -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 192k "output.mp4"
Why this works:
Background becomes a scaled/cropped full-frame layer + blur (hides bars)
Foreground stays sharp and correct aspect (no stretching)
Output is always clean 9:16
